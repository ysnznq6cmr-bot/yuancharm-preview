About yuancharm crystal bracelets

Every yuancharm natural crystal bracelet is hand-finished and prepared before shipping. From warm citrine to grounded obsidian, each edit is organized around a different color, material, or personal intention.

Natural crystals are formed over time, so each bead has its own color and texture. Browse by intention—such as wealth, connection, protection, career, calm, or clarity—or shop by crystal. Best sellers and new arrivals make it easy to discover customer favorites.

Frequently asked questions

How should I care for a crystal bracelet?

Wipe it with a soft cloth and keep it away from prolonged water, perfume, and direct heat. Store it in a soft pouch when not in use.

Which wrist should I wear it on?

Either wrist works. Choose the side that feels comfortable and suits how you style your jewelry.

When will my order arrive?

U.S. orders of $77 or more ship free. Delivery timing is shown at checkout, and returns are accepted within 30 days.
